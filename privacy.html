1. What the service does

Mustafa AI Customer Service is an automated assistant that answers direct messages sent to the test Instagram account mustafa_ai_test, which simulates a computer shop on Instagram. It answers questions about product availability and price from that business's own inventory list. It does not confirm orders, take payments, arrange delivery or give medical advice; order requests are passed to the business's staff.

2. Information we receive

When you send a direct message to a connected Instagram account, Meta sends us:

DataWhy it is used
Your Instagram-scoped ID (a number Meta assigns for this app, not your username)To reply to you and keep your conversation separate
The ID of the business account you messagedTo answer for the right business
Message ID and timeTo avoid answering the same message twice and to ignore old messages
The text of your messageTo understand your question and prepare the answer

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, location, photos or contacts. Images, voice notes and other attachments are not processed and receive no automated reply.

3. Who processes it

ProviderWhat it receivesPurpose

Google — Gemini APIThe text of your message and the business's product listWorking out which product you mean. Prices and stock are never taken from the AI; they are read from the business's inventory

During this test phase the project uses the free tier of the Google Gemini API, and only messages from the project’s own authorised test accounts are sent to it. Under Google’s terms for unpaid services, submitted content may be used by Google to improve its products, so this tier will not be used for real customers’ messages.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles. A request to delete your data is handled without sending it to the AI.

4. What we keep, and for how long

  • Conversation context (your scoped ID, IDs of products you recently asked about, recent message IDs, timestamps — never the text of your messages) is kept in a data table. It is used for 15 minutes after your last message, for follow-up questions, and the row is deleted automatically after 30 days without activity.
  • Operational log (time, processing stage, result and a one-way reference derived from the message ID — never your message text or your ID) is deleted automatically after 30 days. If a message is rejected because the sender is not an authorised tester, that sender's scoped ID is logged so the operator can recognise test accounts; it is deleted on the same schedule.
  • Successful processing runs are not stored in the automation platform's history. Runs that fail with a technical error are stored for troubleshooting and may contain the message; they are kept for at most 30 days (we delete them manually if the platform has not already removed them).
  • Your messages also remain in the Instagram conversation under Meta's own policies.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

Send "احذف بياناتي" or "delete my data" to the same Instagram account and your stored conversation context is deleted immediately; you receive a confirmation with a reference number. You can also email mustafa2005safawi@gmail.com. Full instructions: https://safawi2005aiden-gif.github.io/mustafa-ai-cs-legal/data-deletion.html. Requests by email are completed within 30 days.

6. Security

Messages are accepted only when they carry a valid Meta signature. Access keys are kept in the automation platform's encrypted credential store, not in workflow code. Only the operator can access the systems.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, the minimum age for using Instagram.

8. Changes

We will update this page when the service changes and show the new effective date at the top.
